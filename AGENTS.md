Context for Codex and other coding agents working in this repository. Mirrors `CLAUDE.md` — keep the two in sync.

## What this project is

A single-page, map-based dashboard of the 2026 Bundibugyo ebolavirus (BVD) outbreak in DRC, with the other countries reporting confirmed cases (Uganda, Kenya, Nigeria). It is **plain HTML/CSS/JS**, split into a reusable engine (`index.html` shell, `styles.css`, `app.js`) and one outbreak config (`config.drc-bvd-2026.js`) — see [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md). Leaflet is vendored in `vendor/leaflet/`; there is no Chart.js.

- There is **NO framework** and **NO build step**. The site runs by opening `index.html` directly in a browser.
- **Do NOT** convert it to React (or any framework), and **do NOT** add a bundler, package manager, or build tooling. Keep it as vanilla HTML/CSS/JS loaded with plain `<script src>` tags (no `fetch()` of local files, so `file://` keeps working).

## Current design — "Epidemic Intelligence" (redesigned)

The dashboard was rebuilt to a new layout ("Epidemic Intelligence · Bundibugyo 2026"): a full-bleed map with a floating map-indicator selector, search box, log-scaled legend and a Latest-only time bar, plus a right sidebar with a national + provincial overview and a per-zone view (snapshot, trends chart, mobility panel). Leaflet is vendored (`vendor/leaflet/`); charts are hand-drawn SVG (no Chart.js). Key points for future edits:

- **Data model:** all data is a single `const D` object in `config.drc-bvd-2026.js` — `D.zones` (the 65 mapped health zones of the 66 reported `{z,p,c,d,lat,lon,pop,cfr,k}`), `D.geo` (INRB-UMIE archived v1 shapefile polygons for those 65 zones, `properties.z`), `D.cent`, `D.trends` (per-zone daily series, extended with break rows to the 04 Aug and 05 Sept readings, then daily readings 07 Sept–02 Oct from N°116–141, a break row for the unpublished N°142, daily 04–10 Oct from N°143–149, a break row for the held N°150, and daily 12–20 Oct from N°151–159), `D.nat2` (national time series whose **last row is the live N°159 headline**; a row may carry `nd:{ct:[…],sus:[…]}`, the provinces that SitRep records as not reported (ND) for contact tracing / suspects, plus `ctn` when `ct` comes from the report's own named list of transmitting provinces, so the note reads "covers the {n} provinces INSP lists as transmitting; … are not among them"), `D.tl`, and `D.mob`. National render uses `live=false`, reading the N°159 row from `D.nat2` (9704 cases / 4687 deaths / CFR 48.3% / 2595 recovered / 1041 isolation / 79.7% contact tracing and 663 suspects; contact tracing covers the six provinces INSP lists, without Sud-Kivu and Nord-Ubangi). The previous design's `HZ`/`POP` arrays, `fetch()` of the geojson, `applyStaticI18n`, collapsible panels, etc. are gone.
- **Map base:** OpenStreetMap tiles only (Leaflet), desaturated via the `--tile-filter` CSS. The design's vector base and the Vector/OSM switcher were removed at the maintainer's request; `basePane` is hidden and country labels are a no-op.
- **Mobility is populated from Flowminder / Vodacom, April 2026** (integration test). `D.mob` is built by `tools/build_mobility.js` from the committed mirror `data/mobility_flowminder_od.csv`, extracted from the INRB-UMIE open-data release by `tools/extract_flowminder_od.js`. Schema `D.mob[zone]={tin,tout,in:[[orig,est,lo,hi]…],out:[[dest,est,lo,hi]…],inSup,outSup}`, directed and covering only the original 51 mapped zones (**Mangala**, **Vuhovi**, the 13 zones mapped on 8 Oct 2026 and Wapinda have no Flowminder coverage). Never invent values; `redacted (count <15)` flows are suppressed and never counted as 0; keep the lower/upper bounds and the Flowminder / Vodacom provenance visible. Add a future month by appending rows to the mirror CSV and re-running the adapter — do not rewrite the mobility UI. (See CLAUDE.md for the full rules.)
- **i18n (EN / FR):** the 🌐 button toggles English ⇄ French. Strings live in a `T={en,fr}` dictionary; static markup carries `data-i18n` / `data-i18n-html` / `data-i18n-ph` / `data-i18n-aria` / `data-i18n-label` and is filled by `applyStaticI18n()`, while the dynamic renderers call `tx(key)`. `setLang()` swaps `LANG`, re-applies static i18n and re-runs the renderers; the choice persists in `localStorage.lang`. **The translate function is `tx`, not `t`** — the render functions already use `t` as a local for the current timeline row, so `t` must not be shadowed. French copy is AI-assisted and needs Francophone review (TODO_KIKI).
- **Methods modal** (`#mmodal`, the "Data & Methods" button) carries the sources, the coverage disclosures (493 "A ventiler" Ituri deaths, excluded from zone-level totals: reported zones sum 9704 / 4194 vs national 9704 / 4687; Dakwa unmapped; the ND rule; the held N°150 in `mm_cov_held`) and, in Sources, the geometry provenance (INRB-UMIE archived v1 shapefile) and the CSV downloads. The **"⚠ Verify against WHO before publishing"** note is in the header meta and the modal.
- 65 of the 66 reported zones are mapped. **Dakwa** (Bas-Uélé, from N°147) has no geometry in the archived v1 shapefile (nor in v8.0) and is named but unmapped. A zone without geometry stays off the map but inside its province total, and the province picker shows the engine's note saying how many of the province's affected zones are mapped (`prov_unmapped`, shown only when fewer are mapped than affected).

The technical notes further below describe the **previous** design (HZ array, POP, per-sitrep GeoJSON fetch, choropleth scale breaks, trilingual i18n, collapsible panels) and no longer match the shipped `index.html`; they remain for historical context and for the `data/` files, which are now the N°131 source of truth.

## Data handling

- The figures are **embedded in `config.drc-bvd-2026.js`** (`D`, `NAT`, `PROV`, `CFG`, `T`) and are **also mirrored in `data/`** as CSVs. A few release labels also sit in the `index.html` shell markup (header meta, stamps, download links).
- If any figures change, **keep all copies in sync** — the config, the `index.html` shell text, and the corresponding file(s) in `data/`. `app.js` holds no figures; if an update needs an `app.js` edit, a value has leaked into the engine.
- **SitRep parity (standing rule).** Every national and province figure the dashboard shows must equal the SitRep exactly as printed: national = page-1 banner, province = the province table (Tableau 1: cases, deaths, printed CFR, zones affected). Province totals live in `PROV` in the config (mirrored in `data/provinces_sitrep<NNN>.csv`) and the engine reads them; it only falls back to summing zones when a config has no `PROV`. **Before applying any update, list every dashboard-vs-SitRep discrepancy and the proposed fix, then fix it.** Never make totals agree by redistributing, estimating or dropping figures: unassigned ("A ventiler") deaths stay out of every zone and are counted only in their province and national totals, and unmapped zones stay off the map but inside their province totals.
- **Never invent or estimate numbers.** Use only figures explicitly published by **each country's own public-health authority**: INSP for DRC, the national ministries of health for Uganda, Kenya and Nigeria. **WHO** (Disease Outbreak News) and **Africa CDC** are cross-checks, not sources for a displayed figure. (Amended 22 Oct 2026 when Kenya and Nigeria were added; it previously named WHO and Africa CDC as the only sources.) Aggregators and news reports (e.g. ebola-tracker.org, AP) are cross-checks only and are never cited. If a number isn't in a cited source, leave it out rather than guessing.
- Keep all outputs **aggregated to health-zone level** (or coarser: province/country). Do not produce or display anything more granular than health zone.
- Keep the **"verify against WHO before publishing"** note visible in the UI. Do not remove it.
- **Two GeoJSON files in `data/` look interchangeable but are not.** `zones_sitrep0XX.geojson` carries per-zone `confirmed_cases`/`confirmed_deaths` and is what the live choropleth `fetch()`es. `zones_geometry.geojson` carries **geometry only** (46 zones, no counts) and is staged for a future CSV-driven refactor — it is **not wired up**. Do not point `fetch()` at `zones_geometry.geojson` without also changing how the choropleth reads counts, or the map will render unfilled with no error.
- `data/zones_timeseries_full.csv` (per-zone) and `data/national_timeseries.csv` (headline figures) are reference series for that refactor and are **not read at runtime**.
