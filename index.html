Health-zone coverage

Ten health zones reported in N°114 are named but not yet mapped — Bafwasende, Biena, Buta, Ganga, Gombari, Kayna, Manguredjipa, Mutwanga, Tshopo and Viadana (23 confirmed cases / 10 deaths). Bas-Uélé remains unmapped as a province for this reason. They are absent from the map, so the 51 mapped zones sum to 6,581 cases / 2,799 deaths.

A further 366 confirmed Ituri deaths are pending classification by health zone (“A ventiler”) and are excluded from zone-level death totals: national deaths 3,175 = 2,809 zone-level + 366. Per-zone case shares use the national total (6,604).

Methods

CFR is confirmed deaths ÷ confirmed cases and is right-censored while cases remain unresolved. Recovered is reported nationally only. Cumulative trends are by SitRep report date, not symptom onset; gaps are missing SitReps and are not interpolated. Mobility flows are not included in this release.
